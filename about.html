Why we exist

The accessory is not an afterthought.

People spend days choosing a phone or laptop — then grab whatever cable is cheapest at the counter. GTS was founded to close that gap. We curate accessories the way premium brands curate their flagships: honest materials, tested construction, and design that respects the device it serves.

From our wholesale tiers for shops and distributors to single-piece retail orders, every price is published, every tier is automatic, and every enquiry is answered by a person — not a payment wall.
