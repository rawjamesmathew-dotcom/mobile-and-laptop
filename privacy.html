Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Contact: samith.gts@gmail.com

1. What we collect

When you submit a contact or order-enquiry form we collect your name, email address, country, optional phone number and your message. Cart contents are stored locally in your own browser. We do not take online payments and therefore never collect card or bank details on this website.

2. How we use it

Your details are used solely to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations, confirm orders and — with your consent — share product updates. We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties.

3. Storage & security

Enquiries are stored in the website's private database, accessible only to authorised GTS administrators. Access is protected by individual accounts, password hashing and session controls. We retain enquiries only as long as needed to handle your request and meet record-keeping obligations.

4. Your rights

You may request a copy, correction or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing samith.gts@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable period.

5. Cookies & local storage

This site uses your browser's local storage to remember your cart and preferences. It does not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
